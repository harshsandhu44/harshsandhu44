## The problem

Most IELTS candidates practise a lot and still don't know why their band won't move. Reading and Listening are easy to self-mark. Writing and Speaking are where scores stall, and getting them marked against the band descriptors usually means paying a tutor and waiting.

bandzen marks all four modules on demand, explains what is holding each one back, and turns that into a study plan for the day.

## What I built

bandzen is a Turborepo monorepo with five apps that deploy separately:

- **The product** (app.bandzen.com): a four-skill diagnostic, practice engines for every module, a full mock test, a 40-lesson curriculum, a study plan you can skip, move, pause and replan, progress charts, and an AI coach.
- **A CMS** for writing and publishing content, with a draft workflow, JSON import and a daily job that generates a draft and its listening audio.
- **A staff app** for institutions: cohorts, assignments, reports and seat billing.
- **The marketing site** and **the docs**.

Shared packages hold the database layer, the AI runtime (with one usage table for cost tracking), pricing and an exam registry that already describes PTE, TOEFL and DET alongside IELTS.

It went from first commit to a live, paid product in about four weeks.

## Key decisions

### Data isolation lives in code, not in database policies

The browser holds no database credentials at all. One module is allowed to import the database client, and every query that touches a user's data takes the user id as its first argument. That puts the whole isolation story in a single file a reviewer can read in one sitting. Answer keys sit in their own table so a careless query can never leak them.

### Mark what you can with code, and the rest with evidence

Reading and Listening are scored by plain, tested functions. Writing is graded against the band descriptors with validated structured output, clamped to half-band steps, and runs after the response is sent so a candidate can close the tab. Speaking is graded from the audio itself, so fluency and pronunciation come from what was said rather than a transcript. Every result is labelled an estimated band.

### Derive, don't store

There are no summary tables. The study plan, trends and insights are recomputed from attempts on every read. A stored plan and the attempt behind it can drift apart. A derived one can't.

### Access is one date comparison

A candidate is Pro when their paid period ends in the future. Cancellations, payment retries and comped accounts all fall out of that without special cases. Only the expensive things (AI marking and coach messages) are metered. Reading stays unlimited, because that's the habit that turns into a paying candidate.
